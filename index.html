A JOURNEY THROUGH TIME

Indian Art Timeline

Explore the evolution of Indian art across different periods.

7000 BCE – 1000 BCE
PREHISTORIC

Prehistoric Art

Early humans created paintings of animals, hunting scenes and daily life on rock shelters.

2600 BCE – 300 BCE
ANCIENT

Indus and Buddhist Art

Terracotta figures, seals, stupas and sculptures developed during ancient Indian civilizations.

300 CE – 1200 CE
CLASSICAL

Gupta and Classical Art

Cave paintings, refined sculpture and temple architecture reached remarkable development.

1000 CE – 1700 CE
MEDIEVAL

Medieval Indian Art

Temple architecture, bronze sculpture, miniature painting and decorative arts flourished.

1700 CE – Present
MODERN

Modern Indian Art

Indian artists combined traditional influences with new styles, ideas and global movements.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Art is the memory of a civilization.

Art Map of India is an interactive educational platform designed to explore the geographical and historical development of Indian art.

From ancient cave paintings to modern artistic movements, this project connects places, people, artworks and traditions through an interactive digital experience.
